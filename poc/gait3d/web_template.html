What you are looking at

The transition clip is the runtime doing the thing the case was built for: an intact body walks, its left leg is removed at 2.0 s, and the player blends over one second to the state fitted for a one-legged body. The root is kept on the floor by construction; the joints move at about twice their steady speed during the blend and the support flips from two feet to one at a threshold, which is the open item in the runtime note.

The body clips show, for each of the seven design bodies, the state a growth run (G7) fitted to that body's physics teacher, played by the runtime, and the teacher itself over the same four seconds. The fit is scored in consumer space (contact timing, stance, joint statistics, orientation, rhythm, reach, phase-binned pose), so the two agree on what a viewer would read from the gait and not frame by frame; the teacher is also irregular from second to second where the runtime is periodic.

Bodies: intact; weak left hip; locked left knee; short left shank; left stump (leg ends at the knee); left leg removed; both legs removed. Frames are 25 a second; the runtime can produce any rate.
