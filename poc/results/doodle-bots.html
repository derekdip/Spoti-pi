Doodle Bots Canvas

Bots drawing one word together on a shared canvas. Each stroke is a real human doodle from the Quick, Draw! bank, edited and drawn by a bot's hand. No bot knows where the figure goes; each believes, and corrects its belief from what it sees. At low chaos they converge; at high chaos one draws the roof where it thinks the house is, one draws a second house, one heard "cat". In the room session, people arrive and bots leave to make room.
