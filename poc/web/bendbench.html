Device and method

requestAnimationFrame deltas quantise to whole vsync periods, so they cannot resolve a couple of milliseconds. Instead each sample draws three frames and then forces the GPU to finish with a one-pixel readPixels, timing that block. The readback stall is identical in every mode, so it cancels out of the comparison against the baseline, though it does inflate the absolute figures a little. Calibration raises the stalk count until the no-bend baseline needs about 10 ms of GPU time, a realistic VR-scale load against a 13.9 ms budget at 72 Hz. The baseline is re-run last; a large drift means the device throttled and the run should be repeated cool.
