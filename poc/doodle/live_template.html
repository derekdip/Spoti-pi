Doodle Bots Studio

Bots draw one word together on a shared canvas, live. Every stroke comes from a real human doodle in the bank, edited and drawn by a bot's hand; no bot knows where the figure goes, each believes and corrects from the canvas. Star the doodles you like in the Bank tab and the bots draw only from those. A seed reproduces a round. The same simulation runs the bot service that joins a game room.
